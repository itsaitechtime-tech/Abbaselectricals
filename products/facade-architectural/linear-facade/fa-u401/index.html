U401 Linear Façade Light with Acrylic Cover, 1 m, 12 W, IP66

A 20 × 44.4 mm aluminium linear with an anti-glare acrylic cover for a soft, continuous line, plus a thickened body for heat dissipation. Comes in single-colour, RGB and RGBW (DMX) for building outlines and hotel frontages.
