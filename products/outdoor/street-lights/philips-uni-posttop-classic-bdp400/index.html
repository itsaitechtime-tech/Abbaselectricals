Uni Posttop Classic BDP400 LED Lantern

Classic lantern-style LED post-top luminaire that retrofits traditional post-tops with street-lighting performance: 30W or 80W, up to 9,600 lm, 3000K or 4000K, symmetric or asymmetric optics. Optional 7-pin NEMA socket with DALI readies it for Interact and smart controls. IP66 / IK09.
